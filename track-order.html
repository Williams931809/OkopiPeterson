      Need help with your order?

      Where do I find my order reference?

      Check the payment confirmation page for your order reference. Enter it with the email address you used at checkout.

      Why hasn’t my tracking updated?

      Tracking updates as Maisonpé processes, packs, and ships your order. Once available, the courier and tracking number appear below the shipment timeline.

      What if I paid and my order still says payment pending?

      Look up the order again after payment verification. Keep your order reference and payment receipt handy if you need to follow up.
